# App screenshot sources

Refreshed 12 September 2026. These are genuine app captures, resized and encoded as JPEG for the website. App content was not redrawn or retouched.

- `coastory-profile-2026-09.jpg`: Lauren’s profile, captured from the Play Store installation of Coastory 0.9.3 (18), `com.velocicoder.coastory`, on a Pixel 10 Pro. Android screenshot demo mode shows 9:41, full battery/service, and hides notification icons. Original capture: 1206 × 2410; website: 660 × 1319. The small scroll keeps every favorite readable above the search button. This view contains no rider photos.
- `rooklet-today-2026-09.jpg`: Rooklet 0.2.0 (3), captured on an isolated iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator with the approved Today/Plan/Lists design. The task titles and categories are fictional screenshot fixtures created only in that simulator. Original capture: 1320 × 2868; website: 660 × 1434. The simulator status bar uses 9:41 and full battery/service.

Lauren approved using her Coastory profile for profile shots and the sample data with verified image licenses for other Coastory website and store shots. Do not publish her ride or park photos as part of this refresh. The other Coastory photographs must match the named ride or park and retain their CC0/public-domain provenance.

The earlier PNG files are retained as historical assets but are no longer used in page templates.
