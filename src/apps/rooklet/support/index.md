## Get in touch

Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}?subject=Rooklet%20support). Rooklet is my own app, so your message comes to me.

## Reporting an issue

A few details can help me understand what happened:

- What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
- Your device and operating system.
- The version of Rooklet you’re using, if you have it handy.

A screenshot can also help. Please leave out any personal information you don’t want to share.

## Ideas and feedback

I’m happy to hear what’s working for you, what feels awkward, or what you wish worked differently. You don’t need to have a solution in mind to send a thought.
